# Craft Kids – shortlist (ages 9–12), 29 Oct 2026

## 1. Build a Balloon Car / *Bygg en ballongbil*
- **Age:** 9–12 · **Time:** 30–45 min · **Mess:** low · **Setting:** indoor
- **Categories:** STEM-light, recycled
- **Materials:** PET bottle, 4 bottle caps, 3 bendy straws, 2 wooden skewers, balloon, rubber band, tape
- **Why it fits:** Junk-to-toy build that kids can race and tweak, with Newton's laws thrown in.
- **Source:** Science Buddies: https://www.sciencebuddies.org/stem-activities/balloon-car
- **Safety:** An adult should pierce the bottle caps (the source uses a hobby knife). Watch the skewer points. Balloons contain latex, so keep them away from younger siblings and anyone with a latex allergy.

## 2. Build a Paper Roller Coaster (marble run) / *Bygg en berg-och-dalbana av papper*
- **Age:** 9–12 · **Time:** 45–60 min (can expand) · **Mess:** low · **Setting:** indoor
- **Categories:** paper, STEM-light
- **Materials:** cardstock/printed template, tape, scissors, marbles, cardboard base
- **Why it fits:** An open-ended engineering challenge with loops and curves that stretches 9–12-year-olds without needing special tools.
- **Source:** Science Buddies: https://www.sciencebuddies.org/stem-activities/paper-roller-coaster
- **Safety:** Marbles are a choking hazard for small siblings under 3.

## 3. Make a Whirlybird from Paper / *Gör en pappershelikopter*
- **Age:** 9–12 · **Time:** 15–20 min · **Mess:** low · **Setting:** indoor
- **Categories:** paper, STEM-light
- **Materials:** printer paper (template or hand-drawn), scissors, paper clips, optional stopwatch
- **Why it fits:** A quick win that turns into a real experiment: add paper clips, time the falls, and redesign the wings.
- **Source:** Science Buddies: https://www.sciencebuddies.org/stem-activities/whirlybird
- **Safety:** Drop it from a safe height only, like a sturdy step stool with an adult nearby. Never lean over a balcony.

## 4. Juice-Carton Birdhouse Feeder / *Fågelmatare av juicekartong*
- **Age:** 9–12 · **Time:** 45 min + paint drying · **Mess:** medium · **Setting:** indoor build / outdoor use
- **Categories:** recycled, nature, seasonal-winter
- **Materials:** empty juice/milk carton, scissors, pencil, ruler, stick or skewer, string, acrylic paint, bird seed
- **Why it fits:** It reuses packaging and gets kids watching birds, which suits a Swedish winter when birds are fed.
- **Source:** BBC CBBC – Blue Peter: https://www.bbc.co.uk/cbbc/thingstodo/bp-bird-house-feeder
- **Safety:** An adult can help with the first cut and with poking the hole. Wash the carton thoroughly. Wash hands after handling bird seed.

## 5. Make a Nature Loom / *Väv en naturvävstol*
- **Age:** 9–12 · **Time:** 30–45 min · **Mess:** low · **Setting:** outdoor
- **Categories:** nature, weaving
- **Materials:** 4 sticks, string, fallen grasses, leaves, flowers, feathers
- **Why it fits:** Free materials, a calm and mindful activity, and it looks different every season.
- **Source:** Scottish Wildlife Trust: https://scottishwildlifetrust.org.uk/resource/make-a-nature-loom/
- **Safety:** Only use fallen material, not living plants. Avoid unknown berries and plants that might be toxic. Wash hands afterwards.

## 6. Friendship Bracelets on a Cardboard Loom / *Vänskapsarmband med kartongvävstol*
- **Age:** 9–12 · **Time:** 30–45 min · **Mess:** low · **Setting:** indoor / on the go
- **Categories:** yarn, recycled
- **Materials:** cardboard circle (cereal box), 7 strands of yarn or embroidery floss (~50 cm), scissors, optional bead/button
- **Why it fits:** A home-made kumihimo disc makes a classic 9–12 activity that is easy to learn, portable and good for giving away.
- **Source:** Red Ted Art: https://www.redtedart.com/friendship-bracelets-with-cardboard/
- **Safety:** None significant. The source mentions an optional paid printable; the free page and video are enough.

## 7. Pop-Up Cards (W-fold, paper-chain & stand-up methods) / *Popup-kort – tre enkla tekniker*
- **Age:** 9–12 · **Time:** 20–40 min per card · **Mess:** low · **Setting:** indoor
- **Categories:** paper, seasonal-any
- **Materials:** A5 card, coloured paper, glue stick, scissors, pens
- **Why it fits:** Simple paper engineering that works for any holiday or birthday, and the source says it suits tweens.
- **Source:** Red Ted Art: https://www.redtedart.com/pop-up-card-how-to-projects/
- **Safety:** None significant.

## 8. Futuristic Robot Mask / *Robotmask av flingpaket*
- **Age:** 9–12 · **Time:** 45 min · **Mess:** medium · **Setting:** indoor
- **Categories:** costume, recycled
- **Materials:** cereal-box cardboard, aluminium foil, yarn, hole punch, craft glue, pipe cleaners, sticky notes
- **Why it fits:** A cheap costume piece made from a cereal box, good for Halloween or dress-up, and kids design their own controls.
- **Source:** PBS KIDS for Parents: https://www.pbs.org/parents/crafts-and-experiments/future-robot-mask
- **Safety:** The source lists a glue gun: use craft glue/tape instead, or have an adult supervise a low-temp glue gun. Make sure eye openings give clear vision.

## 9. Woven Paper Heart Basket / *Flätade julhjärtan*
- **Age:** 9–12 · **Time:** 20–30 min · **Mess:** low · **Setting:** indoor
- **Categories:** paper, seasonal-christmas
- **Materials:** 2 sheets coloured cardstock (A4), printed or drawn template, scissors, glue, optional ribbon
- **Why it fits:** A Scandinavian Christmas tradition that feels familiar to Swedish readers, with a weaving puzzle that challenges 9–12-year-olds.
- **Source:** FirstPalette: https://www.firstpalette.com/craft/woven-heart-basket.html
- **Safety:** None significant.

## 10. Tissue-Paper Papier-Mâché Lantern (Halloween / St. Martin's) / *Lykta av silkespapper och papier-maché*
- **Age:** 9–12 · **Time:** 45 min + 1–2 days drying · **Mess:** high · **Setting:** indoor
- **Categories:** paper, seasonal-autumn, recycled
- **Materials:** balloon, tissue paper (reused gift wrap), flour-and-water paste, small scissors, tape, string, LED tea light
- **Why it fits:** A messy, satisfying autumn project that fits Halloween and the dark-season lantern tradition.
- **Source:** Red Ted Art: https://www.redtedart.com/how-to-tissue-paper-halloween-lanterns/
- **Safety:** Use LED tea lights only, never real candles. Don't dry it on a radiator, as the source warns. Balloons contain latex (allergy/choking risk for small children). Flour paste is non-toxic.
