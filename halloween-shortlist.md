# Craft Kids – Halloween shortlist (ages 9–12)

Status: **shortlisted** (not written). Added to `catalogue.json` with the tag `halloween`. All URLs verified on 2026-10-29.
Discovery only: the original instructions have **not** been copied and will be rewritten in Swedish if they are chosen.

## 1. Fladdermus-bokmärke i origami — *Simple Bat Corner Bookmark (easy origami bat)*
- **id:** `origami-bat-bookmark`
- **Category:** paper, halloween, seasonal-autumn
- **Time:** 15–20 min
- **Materials:** square black paper (approx. 15×15 cm), scraps of white/coloured paper, felt-tip or white gel pen, glue stick, scissors
- **Mess:** Low
- **Indoor/outdoor:** indoor / on the go
- **Why it fits:** Quick, cheap fold that ends up as a useful corner bookmark; good intro to origami and fine for a class or party table. Source is a video, so steps must be written up from the fold itself.
- **Source:** Red Ted Art – https://www.redtedart.com/simple-bat-corner-bookmark/
- **Safety:** Low risk. Only children's scissors needed.

## 2. Fladdermus-piñata av toarulle — *Toilet Paper Roll Bat Piñata / Goodie Bag*
- **id:** `tp-roll-bat-pinata`
- **Category:** recycled, paper, halloween, seasonal-autumn
- **Time:** 30–40 min + paint drying
- **Materials:** empty toilet roll, black paint (acrylic or poster), black card, googly eyes or paper eyes, PVA glue, silver/white pen, tissue paper, string, small treats or non-candy surprises
- **Mess:** Medium
- **Indoor/outdoor:** indoor
- **Why it fits:** Recycled and playful: one bat becomes bunting, a treat box or a mini 'trick or treat' piñata, so kids can choose the outcome.
- **Source:** Red Ted Art – https://www.redtedart.com/easy-halloween-craft-pinata-goodie-bag/
- **Safety:** Acrylic paint stains clothes – use an apron and cover the table. Check treats for allergies (nuts, gelatine); small sweets are a choking risk for younger siblings.

## 3. Spöklik skuggteater — *Making Shadow Puppets (with a Halloween shadow-play spin)*
- **id:** `halloween-shadow-theatre`
- **Category:** STEM-light, paper, halloween, seasonal-autumn
- **Time:** 45–60 min
- **Materials:** torch/flashlight, cardstock or cereal-box card, craft sticks or wooden skewers, tape, scissors, pencil, light wall or white sheet, optional: clear and frosted plastic
- **Mess:** Low
- **Indoor/outdoor:** indoor
- **Why it fits:** Real light-and-shadow science (distance, angle, opaque vs. translucent) wrapped in a ghost/bat/witch story kids perform themselves. Perfect for dark October evenings. Halloween theming is our adaptation; the source is a general shadow activity.
- **Source:** Science Buddies – https://www.sciencebuddies.org/stem-activities/shadow-puppets
- **Safety:** Prefer craft sticks over skewers; if skewers are used, cut off/tape the sharp tip. Don't shine the torch into anyone's eyes. Keep the floor clear when the room is dark.

## 4. Halloween-mobil — *Halloween Mobile*
- **id:** `halloween-mobile`
- **Category:** paper, halloween, seasonal-autumn
- **Time:** 45–60 min
- **Materials:** cardstock, crayons/markers/colour pencils, glue, thin string, scissors, hole punch, optional beads, stick or embroidery hoop to hang from
- **Mess:** Low
- **Indoor/outdoor:** indoor
- **Why it fits:** Open-ended drawing of pumpkins, bats, ghosts and cats, plus a little balance problem when hanging it – a decoration kids are proud to put up.
- **Source:** FirstPalette – https://www.firstpalette.com/craft/halloween-mobile.html
- **Safety:** Low risk. Skip the optional hot-laminate step (or adult does it). Hang out of reach of toddlers – string is a strangling risk for small children.

## 5. Häxhatt av papper — *Witch's Hat (paper)*
- **id:** `paper-witch-hat`
- **Category:** paper, costume, halloween, seasonal-autumn
- **Time:** 45 min
- **Materials:** large black paper or thin card (approx. 60×90 cm), black and yellow felt or craft foam, scissors, pencil, ruler, glue, compass or string-and-pencil
- **Mess:** Low
- **Indoor/outdoor:** indoor
- **Why it fits:** A real costume piece with real maths: measuring head size, drawing circles and a cone. Kids end up with something wearable for a Halloween party.
- **Source:** FirstPalette – https://www.firstpalette.com/craft/witch-hat.html
- **Safety:** Compass point is sharp – use carefully or swap for a string-and-pencil 'compass'. No other hazards.

## 6. Spindelnät av glasspinnar och garn — *Halloween Spider Web Craft (with popsicle sticks)*
- **id:** `craft-stick-spider-web`
- **Category:** yarn, weaving, halloween, seasonal-autumn
- **Time:** 30 min + glue drying
- **Materials:** 3 craft/popsicle sticks, yarn (black or white), white glue, black paper, googly eyes, scissors
- **Mess:** Low
- **Indoor/outdoor:** indoor
- **Why it fits:** Calming over-under wrapping that trains fine motor skills, and a nice hanging decoration. Easy to extend: real spider-web facts (orb weavers vs. tangled webs).
- **Source:** Easy Peasy and Fun – https://www.easypeasyandfun.com/halloween-spider-web-craft/
- **Safety:** Source suggests a glue gun as an option – use white glue instead, or an adult handles the glue gun. Otherwise low risk.

## 7. Lysande pumpa av strömdeg — *Squishy Circuits: light-up dough pumpkins and ghosts*
- **id:** `squishy-circuit-pumpkin`
- **Category:** STEM-light, halloween, seasonal-autumn
- **Time:** 30–45 min (+ 20 min if making the dough)
- **Materials:** conductive dough (store-bought play dough works) and insulating dough or modelling clay, 4×AA battery holder with wires, AA batteries, jumbo LEDs, optional: orange/black food colouring
- **Mess:** Medium
- **Indoor/outdoor:** indoor
- **Why it fits:** Kids sculpt jack-o'-lanterns and ghosts that light up while learning circuits, short circuits and LED polarity. The strongest STEM item, but needs a few purchased parts (battery holder + LEDs, or a kit).
- **Source:** Science Buddies (activity) + Science Buddies Blog (Halloween version) – https://www.sciencebuddies.org/stem-activities/squishy-circuits (Halloween version: https://www.sciencebuddies.org/blog/electronics-and-play-dough-fun-tactile-family-science)
- **Safety:** Adult check needed: never let the battery-pack wires touch each other (short circuit, wires get hot). Low-voltage AA only – never mains power. Dough is salty and not for eating. Not a free-material project: needs a battery holder and LEDs.

## Overlap with existing projects

- **tissue-paper-lantern** (existing, Halloween/autumn): no new lantern or candle project was added. The shadow theatre also uses a torch, not a flame.
- **robot-mask** (existing, costume): the witch hat is the only new costume item, and it uses a different technique (paper cone with a brim, not cardboard + foil).
- **nature-loom / cardboard-loom-bracelets** (weaving): the spider web uses a similar over-under winding, but with a different form and purpose (a hanging decoration). Mild technique overlap; drop it if you want less weaving.
- **paper-whirlybird / pop-up-cards** (paper): the bat bookmark is folding (origami), not cutting/pop-up. No duplication.
- **balloon-car / paper-roller-coaster** (STEM): shadow theatre (light) and squishy circuits (electricity) cover new STEM areas.
